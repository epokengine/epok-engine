# Splash artwork

`epok-splash.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The
application overlays the existing `epok-lockup.png`; the logo and live status
are separate from the artwork. The asset is embedded in the executable.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final background artwork for a compact desktop game engine splash screen, landscape 16:9, 1536x864. Primary request: striking abstract retro gaming interpreted with modern cinematic precision, matching Epok Engine's brushed silver metallic geometric brand. Scene: sculptural low polygon metallic prisms and fragmented voxel cubes assembling into an impossible polygonal gateway, suspended over a subtly dithered wireframe terrain, a sense of early 3D console worlds being born. Art directed and restrained: charcoal black, graphite, brushed aluminum silver with electric cyan edge light and small violet glints. Hero geometry concentrated in the right half, dramatic depth and sharp silhouettes; left half and lower quarter remain dark and calm negative space for the existing logo and live loading text that the application will overlay. Fine PS1 era ordered dithering and discreet CRT scanline texture meet premium modern volumetric lighting; no literal game controllers, no characters. No text, no letters, no logo, no loading bar, no mockup border, no watermark. Full bleed finished artwork.
